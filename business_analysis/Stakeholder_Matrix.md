# Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Decision need | Required view | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Management | Where to invest for growth | Executive scorecard, scenarios | Approve priorities and weights |
| Marketing / Recruitment | Where to spend and which channel strategy | Province ranking, campaign simulator | Own campaign assumptions |
| Admissions / Registrar | Expected application and registration volume | Funnel outputs | Validate conversion definitions |
| Finance | Cost, fee-value and budget implications | Scenario economics | Validate cost and fee assumptions |
| Academic Deans | Programme capacity and market relevance | Province + programme layer | Confirm feasible programme growth |
| ICT / Data | Data refresh, lineage and controls | Source register, SQL model | Maintain pipeline and access controls |
| Quality Assurance | Evidence, definitions and governance | KPI dictionary, traceability | Review definitions and limitations |
