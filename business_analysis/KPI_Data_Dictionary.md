# KPI & Data Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Source | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Population 2022 | Revised provincial de jure population | ZamStats 2022 Census | Market-size proxy, not eligible-student count |
| Population growth % | `(Population 2022 / Population 2010 - 1) × 100` | Derived from ZamStats Census | Does not isolate tertiary-age cohort |
| Employment-to-population ratio | Employed population as % of working-age population | ZamStats 2023 LFS | Used as working-adult market proxy |
| Poverty gap ratio | Average shortfall of poor population from poverty line | ZamStats 2022 LCMS | Affordability-pressure proxy, not income |
| Competition context | High for Lusaka/Copperbelt/Central line of rail, lower elsewhere | HEA 2023 | Ordinal, not exact institution count |
| Opportunity score | Weighted normalised composite of five market indicators | Analyst model | Decision-support score only |
| Estimated leads | Campaign budget / cost per lead | Scenario assumption | Requires actual campaign data in production |
| Estimated applications | Leads × lead-to-application rate | Scenario assumption | Not historical performance |
| Estimated registrations | Applications × application-to-registration rate | Scenario assumption | Not historical performance |
| Cost per registration | Campaign budget / estimated registrations | Derived | Sensitive to conversion assumptions |
| Gross fee value | Registrations × selected published fee amount | Scenario | Not revenue recognition or profit |
